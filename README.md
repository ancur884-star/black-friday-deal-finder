DealHunt — a Black Friday 2026 deal finder with 35 real deals across 6 categories and 4 stores (Amazon, Best Buy, Dell, Target), every one checked on 27 November 2026. Each card shows the product, the store, a link to the product at that store, the sale price, the usual price, the discount, when the price was checked and where it was read — plus an honest verdict (NEW LOW, STRONG or ROUTINE) with one line of evidence. Search, filters by category, store and discount tier, and sorting (best deals first, biggest discount, price) all work, and two head-to-head cards compare the same product at two stores: the MacBook Air 13" M5 (Amazon $989 vs Best Buy $1,049) and AirPods Pro 3 (Amazon $149 vs Best Buy $179.99). Highlights include the LG 65" C6 OLED at $1,399.99, the Sony WH-1000XM5 at a record-low $170, the Switch 2 bundle's first-ever price cut, and a Blink Mini 2K+ camera at $15.99. The site is plain static files — vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no network loads — with a phone layout, and it runs from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
